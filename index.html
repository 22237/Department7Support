Remix Your YouTube Playlists

UMixer is an iOS application designed to seamlessly connect to your personal YouTube account, letting you remix existing YouTube playlists sequentially or randomly.

How It Works

1. Secure Sign-In

Log into your YouTube account safely using official Google OAuth authentication.

2. Fetch Playlists

View your saved playlists inside the iOS interface.

3. Custom Remixing

Combine videos from multiple playlists into a new playlist.

4. Watch Anywhere

Access your created playlists directly on YouTube.
