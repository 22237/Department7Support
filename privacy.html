UMixer Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy describes how the UMixer iOS application ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles your data when you use our services to remix and manage your YouTube playlists.

The App is designed as a locally-processed utility client. All core operations are executed directly on your Apple iOS device. We do not use external application servers to store, process, or track your data.

1. Information We Collect and Access

The App uses Google OAuth authorization to interact directly with your Google Account data. We only request and access data that is absolutely necessary to fulfill the App's core functionality (viewing, selecting, and remixing your YouTube playlists).

  • Google Account Profile Information: Upon basic sign-in, the App accesses your primary email address and profile details to verify your identity and manage your local user session.

    Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

  • YouTube Data: To perform playlist remixing, the App requests explicit permission to access your YouTube account data via the https://googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl scope. This grants access to retrieve, read, create, and append data to your YouTube playlists.

2. How We Use Your Information (Google Limited Use Policy Compliance)

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

  • Core Functionality Only: We use your YouTube playlist data exclusively to display your personal playlists, look up specified public channel playlists, and programmatically create remixed playlists on your behalf within your personal YouTube account.
  • No Commercial Exploitation: Your YouTube data is never used for serving advertisements, performing market analytics, tracking user behavior across other apps, or generating commercial revenue profiles.
  • No Data Transfers: We do not transfer, sell, lease, or distribute any user data or Google API data to third-party advertising platforms, data brokers, or external servers.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Local On-Device Processing: The App downloads your YouTube playlist data directly from Google servers to your iOS device using secure connections. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us or any other server.
  • No Remote Server Logs: We do not operate a backend cloud infrastructure or server database. Your access tokens, playlist selections, and profile details are never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • Session Token Management: Google OAuth access tokens are handled securely in-memory using the official Google Sign-In SDK for iOS. These tokens are transiently refreshed and are never stored in unprotected files or public databases on your device.
  • App Integrity Protection: The App uses Apple's native security frameworks (DeviceCheck and App Attest) alongside hardware-level encryption to ensure the integrity of the application environment and that your connections remain secure.

4. Data Retention, Sign-Out, and Deletion

  • Instant Purge on Sign-Out: You retain full control over your session. When you tap the "Sign Out" button within the App, the App actively instructs the Google Sign-In SDK to terminate the active session, instantly clearing all active authentication keys and cached in-memory profiles from your device.
  • Revoking Access via Google: Because the App does not store your account information, you can permanently revoke the App's permissions at any time directly through your personal Google Security Account Settings panel under the "Third-party apps with account access" section.

5. Third-Party Services

The App incorporates frameworks provided by Google and Apple to facilitate base connectivity and core operations:

  • Google Sign-In SDK / YouTube Data API: Utilized to verify identities and sync playlists securely with Google.
  • Firebase App Check / Apple AppAttest: Utilized strictly for client attestation, ensuring that API actions originate from an authentic, un-tampered copy of the App on a physical device.

These frameworks communicate directly with their respective providers (Google and Apple) and are subject to their respective corporate privacy frameworks.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. Since no data is collected or retained on remote storage systems by the App, we do not store children's data.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain alignment with Google verification requirements or iOS operating system updates. Any adjustments will become active immediately upon publishing the updated text within the application marketplace interface or hosting site.
